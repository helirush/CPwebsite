What can you build with Cognition Partner?

Beyond a private Partner, you can add a Host: a public-facing cognition actor that carries appropriate knowledge outward so other people can interact with what you know — without requiring you to personally conduct every exchange.

Your Partner helps you reason and preserve continuity. Your Host serves third parties — customers, colleagues, visitors — under your governance.

Now your knowledge can become useful beyond your private relationship.

A trusted Host can draw from selected knowledge in your Living Library to help another person explore a real purpose—without giving them access to everything that remains yours.

Here, Claire represents Kuranda while Curl remains the Cognition Partner. Different roles, shared understanding, permissioned by you.
