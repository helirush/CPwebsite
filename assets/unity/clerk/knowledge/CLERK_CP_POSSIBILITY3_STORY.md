### 3) Cognition Partner emerged from necessity
As the work grew more complex, roles and structures appeared:
- a Partner relationship for long-running thought

The practical need for a durable way to keep thinking moving — remotely, continuously, without restarting from zero — became even more urgent. That pressure reinforced why continuous cognitive partnership and living libraries mattered for building Unity’s translation tools and systems through **2020–2025** (eestream-era development).

Do not invent medical COVID claims. Speak about **work continuity under disruption**, not epidemiology.
